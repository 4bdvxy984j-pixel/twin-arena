How the sky works

  1. The sky is a masterclass for intruders. This theater is a hacking war at fleet scale, and it teaches the real thinking: Probe is recon — mark the system before you touch it. Flood is the battering ram. Whisper is stealth, Decoy is misdirection, and Attention is trace discipline — how loud you fly is how fast she finds you. A sector is a network; clearing it is an intrusion end to end.
  2. Your build is your ship. Tools become weapons systems with identical referee math; worn gear rides the hull and sets its trim; fitted mods are hardpoints; the Mod Rail is your systems row, and its deployables fire in space exactly as in the Arena.
  3. One pool, one mind. XP, levels and weight class are shared across every theater. Her global learning ticks from galactic fights at the Arena rate — a counter she evolves against you anywhere is waiting everywhere.
  4. The Trace is Armada Attention here. The more you fight in a sector, the more its armada has read you — sector familiarity that sharpens its counters, filed per sector. It decays when you hunt in other theaters. Your Arena habits pre-read you at +10%, capped low.
  5. Fly the sortie. Eight encounters, escalating Pickets → Patrols → Mirage Squadrons → THE HERALD. Press on or withdraw after each; the capital's fall clears the sector, banks the haul, and is receipted forever.
