THE DESCENT

Beneath the arena there are stairs, and at the bottom of the stairs there is loot, and between you and the loot there is the Twin — on her home ground, learning your build as you go.

Crack each depth's gate and choose: descend for better drops and a meaner dark, or ascend and bank what you carry. Your integrity and your Trace carry across the whole run. Bosses guard every third depth and pay the best loot in the house; every fifth depth is a Vault — no gate, no strikes, only fleeing shapes and drop rolls. Death takes your unbanked STURDY and RARE pieces; EPIC, LEGENDARY and GODFORGED always come home. Solo and asynchronous, server-refereed like every bout here — no live PvP, and the loot counts on this page are the real books.

And she is learning you, specifically: lean on a tool — above all a LEGENDARY one — and the deeper you go, the more she scrambles it on sight. Stated plainly, like everything in this house.
