How it works

  1. Fight. Four tools, one gate, two and a half minutes — and the gate fights back. It strikes on a rhythm: parry the haymaker when it winds up and the gate hangs open, vulnerable.
  2. Mind the trace. Loud tools spike your trace; quiet play lets it cool. At 100% you are caught, mid-swing, whatever the gate looks like.
  3. Teach. Your patterns are fingerprinted. Repeat them and they weaken — it has seen them.
  4. Evolve. Spend mutation points down the web so your next fight hits different.
  5. Level up. Fighting earns XP; levels raise your weight class — Spark, Ember, Flare, Blaze, Inferno. XP is a track, never a currency: it cannot be spent or bought.
  6. The Pit. Every member's Twin stands as a devil in the Pit. Fight other fighters' devils — they play their owners' way, and yours defends while you sleep. High-class devils carry the Mirage, and the top class wears Morph Armor. Bounties grow on the unbeaten.
  7. Challenge. Send your Twin — the one hardened against you — to fight your friends.

A game about Gatekeeper's Twin — the sparring partner that learns. Every fight here teaches it a shape; every shape makes the next fighter's road harder. Play is asynchronous: you fight the Twin other fighters educated, and other fighters will meet the one you educate. No live player-versus-player — the opponent is the Twin, and it is enough.
