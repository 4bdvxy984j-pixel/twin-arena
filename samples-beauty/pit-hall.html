⚔ FIGHT THIS DEVIL

The flavors of trouble

MIRAGE-marked — from the Flare class up, a devil lies: some wind-ups are phantoms, and a parry thrown at one bites air. THE MIRROR WRAP LEGENDARY — Steven’s own design: wrap your strike in a mirror and the Mirage has to look at itself. False wind-ups crack; phantom gates ring hollow.

ARMORED — Inferno-class devils wear Morph Armor: at half integrity it morphs once, shedding most of what it knew of your tools and changing its rhythm mid-fight. ANCHOR STITCH RARE — One tool, stitched to your hand before the fight. When the armor morphs and sheds what it knew of you, this one lesson stays sewn in.

THE SECOND HEART EPIC — Declared before a fight. The first time your integrity would hit zero, the Heart breaks instead — and you stand back up at 40. Unbroken Hearts return to your satchel. Earned only off Armored devils, or by a devil that holds five times running for its sleeping owner.

THE STILLBELL EPIC — Declared before a fight, rung from the deck when the trace climbs: for 12 seconds the trace cannot rise — cooling still runs. Unrung Bells return to your satchel. Earned only off high-class Mirage devils.

THE FOUNDER'S EMBER FOUNDER — Granted to the first 100 claimed fighters, then never minted again. A keepsake with a gentle edge: +10% XP on its holder’s fights, and the ember mark on their devil’s card. Passive, permanent, never spent.

Items are earned only — breaching trait devils drops them (the Wrap rarely; it is a legendary thing), and a devil's hold occasionally earns its owner the matching piece. They are numbered editions, signed · the Keeper of the Arena. Never sold, never bought.

A game about Gatekeeper's Twin — the sparring partner that learns. Every fight here teaches it a shape; every shape makes the next fighter's road harder. Play is asynchronous: you fight the Twin other fighters educated, and other fighters will meet the one you educate. No live player-versus-player — the opponent is the Twin, and it is enough.
